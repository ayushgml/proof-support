Your behaviour
is yours.

This policy describes Proof: Daily Accountability, developed by Ayush Gupta, for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

The App Store release does not collect data for the developer. Proof has no account, advertising, tracking or configured analytics-upload service. Your goals, notes, progress, scores and schedules are processed on your devices.

Local records

Proof stores the information you enter and the results it calculates in local app storage. Small summaries are shared with its widgets and extensions through an Apple App Group. Its Apple Watch companion exchanges your day’s summary and actions with your paired iPhone through Apple’s WatchConnectivity. Proof does not provide developer-operated cloud storage or account sync. Device backups are governed by your Apple settings.

Apple Health

If you enable a relevant goal and grant permission, Proof reads the Health data needed for that goal, such as steps, workouts or activity. Where you explicitly enable recording, it can write sessions created through Proof. Health data is used locally for progress and is not uploaded to the developer, sold, or used for advertising. Manage access in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

Screen Time

With your authorisation, Proof uses Apple’s Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings services for the apps or categories you select. Apple provides privacy-preserving selection tokens and usage information to authorised app components. Proof does not read messages, browsing contents or what you do inside another app. Screen Time data is not sent to analytics or data brokers. Restrictions require your authorisation and an enabled rule.

Calendars and notifications

If you connect a calendar, Proof reads enabled calendar events locally to help plan around existing commitments. Notifications and reminders are scheduled on your device according to your settings. These features do not require a developer-operated server. You can revoke access in iOS Settings.

Diagnostics and local analytics

Proof keeps limited diagnostic and usage-event records locally to support troubleshooting. Usage events describe actions and counts rather than goal titles, notes, health values or Screen Time contents. This release has no configured transport to upload analytics. You can manage analytics in Settings. A diagnostic report or data export leaves the app only when you choose to share it. Apple may independently process crash or usage diagnostics under your Apple privacy settings and Apple’s policies.

Export, deletion and support

You can export your records or delete Proof’s local data from Settings. Previously shared exports and operating-system backups must be managed at their respective destinations. If you contact support or create a public GitHub issue, the information you choose to provide is processed by that service and the developer to answer your request. Please avoid including private goals or health data in a public issue.

Website

This support site is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no advertising, tracking scripts or developer analytics. GitHub may process technical request information according to its own privacy policy.

Changes and questions

If a future release changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated. For privacy questions, use the Proof support page; do not include sensitive records in public posts.
