Daily accountability

Make room for
what matters.

Proof brings goals, daily planning, focus sessions and progress into one place. Start with what you want to work toward, choose today’s work, then take the next step.

Need help?
Ask a question or report a problem. Include your app version, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Please keep health information, personal goals and other private details out of public reports.

Getting started

Choose the areas that matter to you and add a goal. When Proof asks what you are taking on today, select the work you want to commit to. Timing is optional: use the schedule when you want a time block, or keep work untimed.

Tasks and focus

Open a goal to add steps, notes and substeps. Use Focus for a work session, and choose the goal or step that session belongs to. You can also log progress from Today.

Your score

The score reflects the work planned for that day. Open the score to understand its inputs. An unscored day shows a dash. Missing integration data reduces confidence instead of counting as failure.

Permissions are optional

Health, calendars, notifications and Screen Time are controlled by your choices. You can use manual logging and the focus timer without connecting Apple Health. Manage enabled connections in Proof’s Settings and in iOS Settings.

Export or delete

Open Settings → Your data to export your local records. Settings also offers deletion of Proof’s local data. An export goes only to the destination you choose.

Apple Watch

Open Proof on your paired iPhone to refresh the Watch’s copy of your day. The iPhone owns the records; Watch actions are sent back to it. A disconnected Watch may show the last available state.
