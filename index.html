What is Proof?

Proof is a personal accountability app for choosing goals, planning your day, focusing on a task, understanding progress and receiving useful nudges. It’s built for iPhone and iPad, with an Apple Watch companion.

Do I have to schedule every task?

No. Give tasks a time if a calendar helps you, or leave them untimed. You can plan your day as a list, a timeline or a weekly grid.

Can I use it for hobbies?

Yes. Creative projects, learning and personal interests belong alongside work and fitness. You decide what goes into today’s plan.

Do I need to connect Apple Health?

No. You can log progress manually and use focus sessions. Health, calendar, notification and Screen Time permissions are optional and remain under your control.

Does Proof use AI or upload my notes?

Proof 1.0 does not use an AI service or upload your goals and notes to the developer. It works locally on your devices. Apple Watch exchanges summaries with your paired iPhone. Apple’s device backups follow your Apple settings.

Is it available on the App Store?

Proof is available now on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, with an Apple Watch companion. It is free, with no active paid subscription. Download Proof.
