Questions about Proof

How do I edit a goal or its steps?

Open Goals and select the goal. Its detail view lets you edit the goal and manage steps, substeps and notes. Choose difficulty and priority, and reorder steps to match how you want to work.

Where do I plan my time?

Open the schedule from Today. You can use a list, daily timeline or weekly grid. Timing is optional, and you can adjust the plan as your day changes.

How do I focus on a particular step?

Open Focus and choose the goal or step the session belongs to. Start the session, pause when needed, and finish it to record the time you put in.

Why does my score show a dash?

A dash means the day is unscored. Proof does not show zero just because data is incomplete. Open a score to understand its inputs. Missing integration data reduces confidence rather than counting as a failure.

Why isn’t Health or Screen Time working?

Check the connection in Proof’s Settings and the permission in iOS Settings or Apple Health. HealthKit and Screen Time require a real device; they do not work in the Simulator. Screen Time also requires individual authorisation, a selected app or category, and an enabled rule. You can still use manual logging and the focus timer. If the connection still fails, contact support; do not share private Health records in a public report.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the iPhone Home Screen, open Edit, then Add Widget. Find Proof, choose a widget and add it. Open Proof to refresh your day’s records.

Why is my Watch showing old data?

Open Proof on your paired iPhone to refresh the Watch’s copy of your day. The iPhone maintains the records, and Watch actions are sent back to it. A disconnected Watch may show its last available state.

Can I export or delete my data?

Open Settings → Your data to export your local records. Settings also offers deletion of Proof’s local data. An export leaves the app only for the destination you choose. Previously shared exports and Apple backups must be managed separately.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No account is required. Version 1.0 is free and has no active paid subscription. Optional integrations can be declined or revoked.
